About

I'm a Lab Manager, Research Assistant, and SSNAP Coordinator at Duke University's Imagination and Modal Cognition (IMC) Lab, working under Felipe De Brigard at the Center for Cognitive Neuroscience. My work centers on how people remember — and specifically, what changes when the visual imagery that usually accompanies memory is diminished or absent, as in aphantasia.

I hold a B.S. in Neuroscience (minor in Chemistry) and a B.A. in Philosophy and Computer Science from the University of Miami (2022), where my undergraduate research spanned transdiagnostic youth mental health treatment and computational moral cognition. I also serve as the Undergraduate Intern Representative for the Cognitive Neuroscience Research Internship (CNRI).

I'm currently preparing applications for MD-PhD and PhD programs in cognitive and clinical neuroscience, targeting Fall 2027 admission.

Research

A selection of the projects I've designed, run, or contributed statistical and operational work to.

Memory Without Mental Images: Aphantasia & Virtual Reality

Duke IMC Lab · PI Felipe De Brigard

A VR-based study dissociating the quantity, accuracy, and metacognitive awareness of episodic recall in individuals with aphantasia and hypophantasia, compared to controls, across immersive kitchen and living-room environments. Presented at CNS 2026 (Vancouver) and Triangle SfN 2026 (Raleigh).

Forgiveness and Autobiographical Memory

Duke IMC Lab · Cross-national collaboration, Colombia & United States

Investigating phenomenological and physiological differences between forgiven and unforgiven remembered wrongdoings, and how reappraisal strategies shape the emotions experienced when recalling autobiographical memories of wrongdoing.

The Moral Machine Project

University of Miami · PIs Geoff Sutcliffe & Debra Lieberman

Senior honors thesis in Philosophy evaluating normative ethical theories through autonomous-vehicle moral dilemmas, including an automated-reasoning presentation on formalizing those dilemmas computationally.

Idiosyncratic Treatment Outcomes in Transdiagnostic Youth

University of Miami · PI Jill Ehrenreich-May

Senior honors thesis in Neuroscience examining idiographic outcome measures for telehealth delivery of the Unified Protocol for Transdiagnostic Treatment of Emotional Disorders in Youth.
